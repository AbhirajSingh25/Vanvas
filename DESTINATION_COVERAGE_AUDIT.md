# VANVAS — Complete Destination & Place Coverage Audit

## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Scope

Prior to this overhaul, the VANVAS catalogue contained 208 seeded places across 26 canonical destinations (exactly 8 places per destination). While functional for an initial prototype, this sparse coverage severely limited discovery for travellers seeking remote Himalayan landscapes or complex heritage cities. Critical landmarks and surrounding regional circuits—such as Tabo Monastery, Pin Valley, Dhankar Lake, and Chicham Bridge in Spiti Valley, or Hemis and Khardung La in Leh—were missing from the core database.

Through this overhaul, the catalogue has been expanded to **420 verified, authentic places** (averaging 16 to 18 places per destination), each equipped with authentic geographic coordinates, price levels, operating hours, curated travel notes (`why_vanvas_recommends`), duration estimates, and authentic visual assets.

## 3. Remote Destination Spotlight: Spiti Valley

Prior to this update, Spiti Valley had only 8 places, missing the most significant monasteries and geographic highlights of the trans-Himalayan high-altitude desert. The catalogue now provides 16 comprehensively mapped landmarks:

1. **Key Monastery** (Immediate / Mid Circuit, 4,166m): Iconic 11th-century cliffside Gelugpa monastery.
2. **Kibber Village** (Nearby Circuit, 4,270m): Historic wildlife and snow-leopard corridor.
3. **Chicham Bridge** (Nearby Circuit, 4,145m): Asia's highest suspension bridge over a 150m gorge.
4. **Langza Village** (Nearby Circuit, 4,400m): Marine fossil village beneath the colossal Golden Buddha statue.
5. **Hikkim** (Nearby Circuit, 4,440m): World's highest functioning post office.
6. **Komic Monastery** (Nearby Circuit, 4,587m): World's highest motorable village monastery.
7. **Dhankar Monastery** (Regional Day Trip, 3,890m): Spectacular 12th-century cliff-edge fortress monastery.
8. **Dhankar Lake** (Regional Day Trip, 4,130m): Glacial high-altitude tarn above Dhankar.
9. **Tabo Monastery** (Regional Day Trip, 3,280m): UNESCO World Heritage 996 CE mud monastery ("Ajanta of the Himalayas").
10. **Pin Valley National Park / Mudh** (Regional Day Trip, 3,800m): Dramatic sub-zero biodiversity reserve.
11. **Sakya Tangyud Monastery** (Immediate Kaza, 3,650m): Major Tibetan cultural centre in Kaza.
12. **The Himalayan Cafe** (Immediate Kaza, 3,650m): Acclaimed travellers' culinary hearth in Kaza market.
13. **Chandratal Lake** (High Pass / Regional, 4,300m): Crescent moon glacial sanctuary.
14. **Kunzum Pass** (High Pass / Regional, 4,551m): Continental divide pass linking Spiti to Lahaul.
15. **Losar Village** (Regional Day Trip, 4,080m): The highest permanent settlement in upper Spiti.
16. **Gue Mummy Temple** (Regional Day Trip, 3,500m): Naturally preserved 550-year-old Buddhist monk Sangha Tenzin.

## 4. Live Discovery Verification & Dynamic Destination Behavior

For dynamically requested destinations not present in the canonical 26-destination database (e.g., Paris, Zanskar, Tirthan Valley, Vagamon):
1. **Live Places Discovery:** The backend invokes OpenStreetMap Overpass and Google Places (when configured) to discover live POIs, compute coordinates, and cluster them into zones.
2. **Live Hotels Discovery:** `LiveHotelsProvider.search_hotels()` retrieves live accommodation, ensuring dynamic destinations never render empty stay sections.
3. **Live Rentals Discovery:** `LiveRentalsProvider.search_rentals()` retrieves local mobility options and motorcycle/scooter rentals.
4. **Visual Art Generation:** If verified exact photography is unavailable, the `CreativeArtProvider` synthesizes or resolves a terrain-appropriate editorial artwork based on elevation and biome.
