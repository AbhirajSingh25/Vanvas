# VANVAS ART BIBLE
## Permanent Visual Specification & Destination Artwork System

---

## 1. Core Visual Idea & Philosophy

```
Illustration = invitation / imagination
Photography  = actual place / discovery
Map          = movement
UI           = control
```

VANVAS is an intelligent modern travel companion designed for travellers who want authentic, unpretentious journeys across the Indian subcontinent and beyond. It is not a tourism-board advertisement, nor a generic SaaS dashboard.

Every visual element must respect this fundamental division:
1. **Destination Discovery & Explore**: Uses **Editorial Landscape Illustration** to spark curiosity, evoke environmental atmosphere, and serve as an invitation into the territory.
2. **Destination Detail & Field Guide**: Uses **Cinematic Real Photography** to reveal the actual place, specific trails, pine cafés, riverside ghats, stone architecture, and stays.
3. **Map & Routing**: Represents terrain, elevations, and movement cleanly with mountain tortuosity awareness.
4. **UI**: Remains clean, restrained, functional, and editorial.

---

## 2. Visual Character & Art Direction

### Key Aesthetic Pillars:
- **Contemporary Indian Editorial Illustration**: Drawing inspiration from high-end travel journalism, vintage Indian railway posters, and modern printmaking.
- **Sophisticated Gouache & Screen-Print Texture**: Subtle tactile paper grain, flat-color layering, and clean ink edges.
- **Layered Environmental Landscapes**: Natural spatial depth created through overlapping mountain ridges, river corridors, tree lines, and mist gradients.
- **Strong Silhouettes & Negative Space**: Memorable compositional shapes (temple spires, deodar pine canopies, suspension bridges, fort ramparts) balanced with expansive skies and river surfaces.
- **Cinematic Atmospheric Depth**: Subtle morning mist, alpine dust, monsoon cloud cover, or desert dusk light without exaggerated fantasy glow.
- **Restrained, Culturally Grounded Palette**:
  - Forest / Deep Valley Green: `#173B32`, `#0F2924`
  - Mountain Mist / Slate: `#D8DED5`, `#273D52`
  - Warm Paper & Parchment: `#EFE5D2`, `#FAF4E8`, `#E5D5BA`
  - Terracotta / Laterite / Sandstone: `#B65E3C`, `#7B4D36`
  - Brass Glow / Harvest Mustard: `#B49252`

### Grounded Indian Identity:
The Indian character must emerge naturally from:
- Real topography (Himalayan valleys, Kangra ridges, Parvati gorges, Western Ghats, Thar sands, Ganges riverbanks).
- Authentic regional architecture (Kath-Kuni timber-and-stone houses, laterite Goan villas, Rajasthani jharokhas and ramparts, tiered Tibetan gompas, river ghat steps).
- Local vegetation (deodar cedar, Himalayan pine, coconut palms, rhododendron, alpine scrub).
- Atmospheric environmental details (river mist, mountain winding roads, chai stalls along the highway, wooden footbridges).
- Selective Devanagari typographic accents in UI labels (never inside the artwork itself).

---

## 3. Mandatory Negative Constraints (What to Avoid)

All destination artwork MUST strictly avoid:
- ❌ **No Text in Artwork**: No destination names, slogans, English/Hindi typography, fake signs, or watermarks. All text belongs strictly in the UI.
- ❌ **No Stereotypical Tourism Clichés**: No random elephants, floating airplanes, passport stamps, generic compass roses, fake handwritten notes, or stock backpacks.
- ❌ **No Generic AI Fantasy Aesthetics**: No glowing neon castles, cartoonish Pixar styles, anime tropes, hyper-saturated fantasy landscapes, or generic vector blobs.
- ❌ **No Monolithic Templates**: Manali, Spiti, Leh, and Mussoorie must not look like the same mountain with a color shift. Each destination has distinct topography, vegetation, and architecture.
- ❌ **No Broken Images or Obvious Placeholder Boxes**: Fallbacks must always be styled editorial landscapes.

## 4. Destination-Specific Composition Blueprints

### A. Seeded Destinations

#### 1. MANALI (Himachal Pradesh)
- **Topography**: Steep U-shaped Beas river valley, high snow-dusted Himalayan peaks in distant background.
- **Vegetation**: Dense pine and deodar forests along valley slopes.
- **Architecture**: Subtle Kath-Kuni wooden houses with slate roofs, stone bridge over river.
- **Atmosphere**: Cool morning mist weaving between pine tiers, mountain road winding upward.
- **Palette**: Deep forest green, slate blue, warm earth terracotta, cool white mist.

#### 2. RISHIKESH (Uttarakhand)
- **Topography**: Emerald-turquoise Ganga cutting through low forested Himalayan foothills.
- **Vegetation**: Lush subtropical foothill forests, cliffside greenery.
- **Architecture**: Silhouette of suspension bridge (Ram Jhula / Lakshman Jhula style), riverside ghat stone steps.
- **Atmosphere**: Early morning river stillness, serene water currents, soft mist over foothills.
- **Palette**: River turquoise, deep foothill green, warm stone ochre, sunrise amber.

#### 3. KASOL & PARVATI VALLEY (Himachal Pradesh)
- **Topography**: Dramatic narrow river gorge, sheer vertical mountain walls, boulder-strewn Parvati river.
- **Vegetation**: Towering deodar cedar canopies, riverside wild growth.
- **Architecture**: Compact wooden mountain cabins nestled against forested slopes.
- **Atmosphere**: Adventurous, moody, misty pine air, white-water river spray.
- **Palette**: Slate grey, moss green, cedar bark brown, emerald water tint.

#### 4. DHARAMSHALA / MCLEODGANJ (Himachal Pradesh)
- **Topography**: Dramatic rising wall of the Dhauladhar range towering immediately above the ridge.
- **Vegetation**: Cedar and rhododendron slopes.
- **Architecture**: Tibetan-influenced multi-tiered monastery roofs, hillside settlement silhouette.
- **Atmosphere**: Moody alpine clouds drifting across rocky crags, serene mountain elevation.
- **Palette**: Dark pine green, slate blue, burgundy/crimson accents, snowy grey.

#### 5. GOA (West Coast)
- **Topography**: Coastal curves, gentle laterite headlands, tidal river inlets, Arabian Sea horizon.
- **Vegetation**: Natural coconut palm groves, dense coastal scrub, mangroves.
- **Architecture**: Portuguese-influenced laterite stone villas, arched windows, quiet coastal roads.
- **Atmosphere**: Warm tropical dusk, long golden sea shadows, laid-back coastal rhythm.
- **Palette**: Terracotta red, deep palm green, warm ochre gold, ocean navy.

#### 6. JAIPUR (Rajasthan)
- **Topography**: Aravalli rocky ridge lines, arid plains, dry scrub terrain.
- **Vegetation**: Sparse desert acacia, date palms in courtyards.
- **Architecture**: Pink-sandstone fort ramparts, palace jharokha geometry, tiered battlements.
- **Atmosphere**: Golden late-afternoon desert haze, strong architectural geometric shadows.
- **Palette**: Muted terracotta pink, desert sand, mustard gold, dusty slate.

#### 7. UDAIPUR (Rajasthan)
- **Topography**: Shimmering Lake Pichola surrounded by rolling Aravalli hill silhouettes.
- **Vegetation**: Lakeside palms, bougainvillea, lakeside gardens.
- **Architecture**: Whitewashed marble and sandstone palace facades rising directly from the water, domed chhatris.
- **Atmosphere**: Evening lake reflections, calm water ripples, regal quiet.
- **Palette**: Ivory stone, lake blue, sunset apricot, hill charcoal.

#### 8. MUSSOORIE (Uttarakhand)
- **Topography**: High east-west mountain ridge overlooking the Doon Valley on one side and snow peaks on the other.
- **Vegetation**: Oak, pine, and deodar forest ridge walks.
- **Architecture**: Sloping colonial-era metal roofs, winding hill cart roads, ridge viewpoints.
- **Atmosphere**: Famous winterline sunset glow, rolling monsoon clouds across valleys.
- **Palette**: Deep hill green, winterline amber-purple, misty navy, warm wood brown.

#### 9. SPITI VALLEY (Himachal Pradesh)
- **Topography**: High-altitude stark cold desert, carved canyon walls, braided Spiti River basin.
- **Vegetation**: Extreme sparse alpine scrub, barren geological strata.
- **Architecture**: Whitewashed mud-and-timber monastery (Key Gompa style) perched atop rocky promontory.
- **Atmosphere**: Vast crystalline high-altitude sky, extreme spatial scale, stark raw beauty.
- **Palette**: Ochre clay, stark grey, sky cobalt, snow white, deep umber.

#### 10. LEH & LADAKH (Ladakh)
- **Topography**: High plateau desert, dramatic barren mountain passes, snow-capped Karakoram and Zanskar ridges.
- **Vegetation**: Willows and poplars in river oasis valleys only.
- **Architecture**: Multi-tiered Tibetan royal palace silhouette (Leh Palace style), stupas along mountain ridges.
- **Atmosphere**: High UV clear blue sky, sharp mountain silhouettes, sweeping high-altitude valley roads.
- **Palette**: Sandstone gold, barren mountain brown, intense sky blue, stark snow caps.

#### 11. VARANASI (Uttar Pradesh)
- **Topography**: Sweeping crescent bend of the sacred Ganges River.
- **Vegetation**: Distant green opposite bank, urban river edge.
- **Architecture**: Dense historic stone ghat steps, multi-layered temple spires, old stone havelis.
- **Atmosphere**: Dawn river mist, soft reflections of brass oil lamps, timeless river stillness.
- **Palette**: River ochre, temple stone red, dawn saffron, river blue-grey.

## 5. Visual Intelligence for Arbitrary Unseeded Destinations

For dynamically discovered destinations (*e.g., Munnar, Meghalaya, Kedarnath, Coorg, Bali, Kyoto*), the system evaluates:
1. **Terrain Classification**: `himalayan` | `high_altitude_desert` | `coastal` | `valley_plateau` | `desert_heritage` | `river_heritage` | `tropical`
2. **Elevation Tier**: High Alpine (>2500m), Foothills (500–2500m), Coastal / Plains (<500m).
3. **Regional Color DNA**:
   - Tea hills / South India: Emerald green, moist mist, laterite earth.
   - High Desert: Ochre, stark grey, cobalt sky.
   - Coastal: Warm gold, palm green, deep navy.
   - Himalayan: Pine green, slate blue, snow white.

The fallback hierarchy resolves gracefully:
```
1. Verified Real Destination Photography (Hero/Card)
   ↓ (if missing or loading)
2. Curated Seeded Destination Illustration (/images/destinations/[slug]/illustration.webp)
   ↓ (if unseeded)
3. Regional High-Quality Visual Artwork (/images/destinations/fallbacks/[region].webp)
   ↓ (emergency safety net)
4. Procedural Editorial SVG Canvas (Harmonized palette & elevation contours)
```

## 6. Creative MCP Interface Specification

To support future programmatic artwork generation through an MCP agent or image synthesis service, the system adheres to this contract:

```typescript
export interface CreativeArtworkRequest {
  destination_name: string;
  slug: string;
  region: string;
  country: string;
  elevation_meters?: number;
  terrain_type: "himalayan" | "coastal" | "desert" | "valley" | "high_desert" | "river_ghat";
  visual_role: "illustration" | "hero_photography" | "place_photography";
  aspect_ratio: "square" | "tall" | "wide" | "hero";
}

export interface CreativeArtworkResponse {
  asset_url: string;
  is_curated: boolean;
  visual_role: string;
  art_direction: string;
  prompt_used?: string;
  validation_status: "verified" | "fallback" | "pending";
}
```

### Prompt Construction Template for Future Art Generation:
```
"Premium contemporary Indian editorial travel illustration of [Destination Name], [State/Region], [Topographic landscape description], [Regional architectural silhouette], [Local vegetation & road/river movement], [Atmospheric lighting condition], sophisticated gouache and screen-print texture, [Harmonious color palette tokens], cinematic composition, premium travel publication art direction, atmospheric depth, elegant and mature, no text, no letters, no watermark."
```

---

## 7. Quality Validation & Performance

- **Aspect Ratio Consistency**: Standardized to `wide` (16:10), `tall` (3:4), `square` (1:1), and `hero` (21:9).
- **Format Standard**: `.webp` preferred, fallback to high-quality `.jpg`.
- **Zero Broken Images**: All `img` tags are wrapped in `VanvasImage` with multi-tier `onError` recovery.
- **Zero Text In Generated Artwork**: Automated validation checks and prompt enforcement ensure no AI gibberish text is baked into image layers.
