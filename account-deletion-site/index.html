What happens when you delete your account?

Submitting this request will permanently and irreversibly erase your entire profile and all personal travel assets from the VANVAS platform:

  • User profile, email address, password hashes, and session authentication tokens.
  • Custom saved itineraries, day schedules, and travel journals.
  • Dynamic Replanner adjustments, pacing history, and GPS waypoints.
  • Travel Ledger entries, expense receipts, split ledgers, and settlement payments.
  • Uploaded profile photos and customized avatars.
  • Saved destination bookmarks, reviews, and travel circle memberships.

To prevent unauthorized requests, please authenticate with your existing account credentials.
